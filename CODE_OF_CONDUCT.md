# Code of conduct

SEO Control Center is for people with very different levels of SEO and coding experience. Treat everyone here with respect.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Accept a maintainer's decision, even when you disagree with it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, including promoting link schemes or SEO services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may block people from the project. To report a problem privately, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/seoccnet/seo-control-center/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct". Reports are kept confidential.
